## 1. Definitions

- **"Platform" / "SecureOps Command"** — the security-operations software-as-a-service owned, developed, and operated by SOBBU, comprising the hosted backend/API, the web admin portal, the public website, and the shared mobile applications (iOS/Android), together with related documentation and updates.
- **"Customer Deployment"** — the dedicated instance provisioned for Customer (its own backend, database, admin/web surface, and branding), reachable through Customer's assigned organization code and/or domain.
- **"Authorized Users"** — individuals Customer permits to access the Platform, including Customer's administrators, dispatchers, site managers, and security officers (whether employees or 1099 contractors of Customer).
- **"Customer Data"** — all data Customer or its Authorized Users submit to or generate in the Platform, including personnel/HR records, payroll and banking details, incident reports, schedules, time entries, chat messages, and location data.
- **"Order Form"** — an ordering document (Exhibit A) referencing this Agreement that sets out the subscribed tier, fees, term, and any deployment specifics.
- **"Sub-processor"** — a third party engaged by SOBBU to process Customer Data in the course of providing the Platform (see Exhibit B).

6.5 **Customer-side security.** Customer is responsible for security within its control, including device security, account/credential hygiene, role assignments, and offboarding.
